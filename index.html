08 / Frequently asked questions
How do we work?

We offer both virtual and physical services, depending on what you have in mind or at hand.

What services do we offer?

GoodLife Tech Start-up is a dynamic and versatile tech company that specializes in websites, applications, cyber security, content writing, robotics, games, teaching, mentoring and troubleshooting.

Why choose us?

We believe in building strong and long-lasting partnerships with clients, understanding business objectives and tailoring solutions to specific needs. We commit to high-quality products, on time and within budget.

What happens if we do not deliver?

It is not expected, but in such a case, the client gets a 25% return and still gets the project or work done. If it cannot be done due to one reason or another, the client gets a 100% return.
