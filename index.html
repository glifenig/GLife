01 / About us

A forward-thinking tech startup

Glife Nigeria Ltd is a technology company focused on developing software that makes business, services and everyday processes simpler. We believe technology is most useful when it addresses a genuine problem. That belief guides the products we build, the clients we work with and the future we are building toward.

Our Story

Glife was built on one simple premise: people have problems, and technology can help solve them.
Today, our focus is software.
We design and develop web platforms, mobile applications and custom digital systems for people and organisations looking for better ways to work, operate and serve their users.
We do not begin by asking which technology we want to use. We begin by asking: What problem needs to be solved?
That question shapes every Glife project.

Mission

We build software and, in time, manufacture hardware products that solve genuine problems for the people and organisations who use them, engineered with excellence, delivered in partnership, and grounded in honesty about what our technology can do.

Vision

To be Africa's trusted technology partner, advancing the digital space so that individuals, business, government, and everyday life become simpler through technology.

03 / How we work

From problems to working products.
Software projects become easier when everybody understands what happens next.

Process 6 stages
No. Stage Approach Note
01 Discover We understand the problem. We learn about your organisation, users, objectives, current process and the problem the technology needs to solve.
02 Define We determine the right solution. Together, we define requirements, priorities, user journeys, project scope and what success should look like.
03 Design We make the experience clear. We plan the structure and user experience before development so the product is intuitive for the people who will use it.
04 Develop We build with purpose. Our development team turns the approved solution into functional, reliable software while keeping the project aligned with its objectives.
05 Test & Launch We make sure it works. We test, refine, and prepare the product for deployment before it reaches your users.
06 Support & Improve Technology keeps evolving. Where required, we continue supporting, maintaining and improving the solution as your organisation grows
08 / Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before starting a project with Glife.

What does Glife do?

Glife designs and develops web applications, mobile applications and custom software solutions for businesses, entrepreneurs, organisations and institutions.

What types of projects can Glife build?

We work on projects including websites, web applications, mobile apps, administrative systems, customer platforms, workflow systems and other custom digital products.

Can you develop an idea that has not been built yet?

Yes. You can come to us with an idea, business challenge or user problem. We can help define what needs to be built before moving into design and development.

Do I need technical knowledge before contacting Glife?

No. You do not need to know programming languages, frameworks or technical architecture. Start by explaining the problem you are trying to solve.

Does Glife work with startups?

Yes. We can help founders move from an early idea through product definition, design, development and launch.

Do you work with established organisations?

Yes. We can help organisations develop new platforms, digitise existing processes, improve customer experiences and replace inefficient systems.

Can you improve an existing application or website?

Yes. We can first review the current product and then determine whether it should be improved, extended, integrated with other systems or rebuilt.

How much does software development cost?

The cost depends on factors such as project complexity, features, integrations, platforms and expected delivery timeline. After understanding your requirements, we define the scope and provide an appropriate proposal.

How long will my project take?

Every project is different. A website may take substantially less time than a multi-user mobile and web platform. A realistic delivery timeline is established once the project requirements have been properly defined.

Will I be involved during development?

Yes. Partnership is an important part of how Glife works. Clients remain involved during requirements definition, design reviews, progress reviews, testing and approval.

Does Glife provide support after launch?

Yes. Glife can provide ongoing maintenance, technical support and further development depending on the requirements of the project.

How do I work with Glife?

Complete the Get Started form and tell us what you want to build or the problem you want to solve. Our team will review the request and contact you about the next step.
